Professional Training

Registered Energy Manager (REM) Type 1 Training

📅 Next intake: 16 – 20 November 2026 📍 UTMSPACE Johor Bahru ⏱️ 5 Days (Full Time)

The Registered Energy Manager (REM) Type 1 is a five-day intensive professional training programme conducted by UTM through UTM SPACE, a registered training institution under Suruhanjaya Tenaga (ST). The programme equips participants with essential knowledge and practical skills in energy management, energy efficiency, energy auditing, and compliance with Malaysia's Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act 2024 (EECA 2024). Key topics include energy policies and legislation, Energy Management Systems (EnMS), energy audits, and Energy Saving Measures (ESMs) for lighting, compressed air, HVAC systems, motors, pumps, and fans.

2026 intakes: 19–23 January · 9–13 February · 4–8 May · 10–14 August · 16–20 November 2026.

Suitable for: Engineers, facility managers, energy consultants, sustainability officers, and professionals involved in energy management and facility operations.

Certification: REM Type 1 certificate issued by Suruhanjaya Tenaga (ST), subject to their requirements and approval.

Fee: RM 4,900 per participant (HRDC claimable rate), RM 4,600 per participant (normal rate)

ICEGJ 2026 Opens in Hanoi as Erasmus+ TUNE Drives ASEAN–EU Green Competency Transformation

26 October 2026, Hanoi: the 1st International Conference and Exhibition on Green Jobs for a Net-Zero Future (ICEGJ 2026) opened at the Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST) under the banner “Aligning Green Skills – Advancing Policy – Transforming Workforces”, as the dissemination milestone of the Erasmus+ CBHE TUNE project (Nurturing Future Green Leaders) and part of HUST’s 70th anniversary. More than 200 delegates from ASEAN and Europe attended; TUNE project coordinator Prof. Ir. Ts. Dr. Mohd Fadhil bin Md Din of UTM delivered the welcoming remarks and the keynote “From Green Skills to Green Impact”, proposing industry-responsive micro-credentials, Training-of-Trainers frameworks and a Green Competency Passport.

Aquaculture

Kelah Project at UTM Pagoh Builds Bumiputera Aquaculture Entrepreneurs

20 October 2026, UTM Kampus Pagoh: a TERAJU-funded freshwater aquaculture project — RM2.1 million under the Dana Pembangunan Usahawan Bumiputera, reaching 15 participants around Johor — operates its ponds at the Innovation Centre in Agritechnology for Advanced Bioprocessing (ICA). Built in August 2024 and stocked with kelah and red tilapia a month later, the site runs a recirculating aquaculture system with UV filtration and IoT remote monitoring; red tilapia yields about 250 kg every three months, while kelah is marketed through a buy-back arrangement with Far East Planet Agrofarm.

Bulan Akademia

Terra Vibes: Turning Glass Jars into Mini Ecosystems

16 October 2026, UTM Johor Bahru: “Terra Vibes: Walk into Mini Nature” — run by the Office of the Deputy Vice-Chancellor (Academic & International) with the School of Undergraduate Studies for Bulan Akademia UTM 2026 — had seven faculties build terrariums layer by layer, from gravel for drainage and activated charcoal as a filter to organic soil, live moss and small plants. The Faculty of Science took top honours, followed by the Faculty of Built Environment & Surveying and the Faculty of Chemical & Energy Engineering, and students from Universitas PGRI Semarang, Indonesia, performed at the closing ceremony.
